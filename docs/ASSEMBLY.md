# SRO Robot Arm Assembly Guide

This document provides step-by-step instructions for assembling the SRO Robot Arm Kit. The robot arm consists of two arm segments with rotating joints and a platform, controlled by a microcontroller with CAN bus expansion capabilities.

                     9. ## Component Procurement
                    
                     10. ### Hardware Components
                    
                     11. **Mechanical Components:**
                     12. - 2x Arm segments (can be 3D printed or machined from aluminum)
                         - - Rotating platform base (can be 3D printed or laser-cut)
                           - - 3x Servo motors (or stepper motors for precise control) - for arm segment rotation
                             - - Fasteners: M3, M4, M5 bolts, nuts, and washers (approximately 50 pieces of each size)
                               - - 2x Bearing sets (8mm ID) for smooth joint rotation
                                 - - Aluminum or steel brackets for mounting (see CAD directory for specifications)
                                  
                                   - **Electrical Components:**
                                   - - 1x Raspberry Pi 4 or compatible microcontroller
                                     - - 1x CAN bus module (MCP2515 or equivalent)
                                       - - 1x 5V power supply (minimum 3A recommended)
                                         - - 1x 3S LiPo Battery (11.1V, 2200mAh or higher)
                                           - - Wiring: 22-24 AWG copper wire (red, black, and colored for signal lines)
                                             - - Connectors: XT60 for power, JST connectors for servo/sensor connections
                                               - - 330Ω resistors (for IR receiver signal conditioning)
                                                 - - 2x IR receiver modules
                                                  
                                                   - **Sensors & Expansion:**
                                                   - - Grabber module (optional, requires CAN interface)
                                                     - - Pi Zero Vision Module (optional, requires CSI ribbon)
                                                      
                                                       - ### Supplier Recommendations
                                                       - - Mechanical parts: Local machine shops, 3D printing services (Shapeways, Prusa), or aluminum suppliers
                                                         - - Electronics: Industry standard suppliers (Digi-Key, Mouser, SparkFun, Adafruit)
                                                           - - Fasteners: Home Depot, Fastenal, or local hardware stores
                                                            
                                                             - ### Estimated Timeline
                                                             - - Parts procurement: 2-7 business days (depending on suppliers)
                                                               - - Total assembly time: 4-6 hours for a first-time builder
                                                                
                                                                 - ## Tools Required
                                                                
                                                                 - **Mechanical Tools:**
                                                                 - - Screwdrivers (Phillips and flathead, multiple sizes)
                                                                   - - Hex key set (Allen wrench set, 1.5mm to 6mm)
                                                                     - - Soldering iron (25-40W) and solder
                                                                       - - Wire strippers and crimpers
                                                                         - - Calipers or ruler (for measurements)
                                                                           - - Dremel or rotary tool (for fine tuning)
                                                                            
                                                                             - **Measurement Tools:**
                                                                             - - Multimeter (for electrical testing)
                                                                               - - Level or straightedge (for alignment)
                                                                                
                                                                                 - **Safety Equipment:**
                                                                                 - - Safety glasses
                                                                                   - - Work gloves
                                                                                     - - Solder fume extractor or ventilation
                                                                                      
                                                                                       - ## Base Platform Assembly
                                                                                      
                                                                                       - 1. **Prepare the platform:** Clean the rotating platform base piece and check for any burrs or imperfections.
                                                                                        
                                                                                         2. 2. **Mount bearing:** Install the bearing set into the platform center axis mounting point. Ensure it rotates freely.
                                                                                           
                                                                                            3. 3. **Install servo motor:**
                                                                                               4.    - Mount the servo motor underneath the platform base
                                                                                                     -    - Align the motor shaft with the bearing axis
                                                                                                          -    - Secure with M3 bolts (3-4 bolts recommended)
                                                                                                               -    - Leave the servo horn loose for now
                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                    - 4. **Attach servo horn:** Once the servo is mounted, attach the servo horn to the motor shaft and secure the platform rotation linkage.
                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                      5. 5. **Check rotation:** Manually rotate the platform to verify smooth operation. It should rotate at least 180 degrees without resistance.
                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                         6. ## Arm Segments Assembly
                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                         7. ### Segment 1 (Base Arm)
                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                         8. 1. **Prepare segment:** Inspect the arm segment for proper dimensions (reference CAD files for exact specifications).
                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                            2. 2. **Mount servo motor:**
                                                                                                                               3.    - Position servo motor at the segment base
                                                                                                                                     -    - Secure with M4 bolts
                                                                                                                                          -    - Servo horn should face toward the joint area
                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                               - 3. **Install rotation joint:**
                                                                                                                                                 4.    - Insert bearing into joint area
                                                                                                                                                       -    - Connect servo horn to joint linkage with appropriate brackets
                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                            - ### Segment 2 (End Effector Arm)
                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                            - 1. Follow the same procedure as Segment 1
                                                                                                                                                              2. 2. Ensure servo alignment matches Segment 1 for synchronized movement
                                                                                                                                                                 3. 3. Test individual segment rotation
                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                    4. ## Joint Assembly
                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                    5. 1. **Prepare joint components:** Align both arm segments at the connection point.
                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                       2. 2. **Install connecting hardware:**
                                                                                                                                                                          3.    - Use M5 bolts for primary connection
                                                                                                                                                                                -    - Install washers on both sides of the joint
                                                                                                                                                                                     -    - Tighten gradually to avoid misalignment
                                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                          - 3. **Install coupling mechanism:** Connect the servo from the platform to the first arm segment joint.
                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                            4. 4. **Test articulation:**
                                                                                                                                                                                               5.    - Manually move the arm through its full range of motion
                                                                                                                                                                                                     -    - Verify no grinding or resistance
                                                                                                                                                                                                          -    - Check that servo can control movement smoothly
                                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               - ## Electrical Assembly
                                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               - ### Microcontroller Setup
                                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               - 1. **Prepare Raspberry Pi:**
                                                                                                                                                                                                                 2.    - Install Raspberry Pi OS
                                                                                                                                                                                                                       -    - Enable SPI and I2C interfaces
                                                                                                                                                                                                                            -    - Install necessary libraries for motor control
                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 - 2. **CAN Bus Module Installation:**
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   3.    - Connect MCP2515 CAN module to Raspberry Pi:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -      - VCC → Pin 2 (5V)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -       - GND → Pin 6 (GND)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -        - MOSI → Pin 19 (GPIO 10)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -         - MISO → Pin 21 (GPIO 9)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -          - SCK → Pin 23 (GPIO 11)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -           - CS → Pin 24 (GPIO 8)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -            - INT → Pin 22 (GPIO 25)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -        3. **Power Supply Connection:**
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -       - Connect 5V power supply to Raspberry Pi micro-USB port
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -      - Connect LiPo battery to servo power rail (through regulator if using higher voltage servos)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -  ### Servo Motor Wiring
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -  1. **Connect servo signals:**
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            2.    - Orange/Yellow wire (control signal) → GPIO pins for PWM control
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  -    - Red wire (power) → 5V power rail
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       -    - Brown/Black wire (ground) → Ground rail
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            - 2. **Power distribution:**
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              3.    - Use power distribution board for clean power delivery
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    -    - Add capacitors (1000µF) across servo power lines to prevent voltage spikes
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         - ### IR Receiver Installation
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         - 1. **Mount IR receivers:** Position on opposite sides of the robot arm for remote control capability.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           2. 2. **Connect to microcontroller:**
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              3.    - Signal pin → GPIO pin with interrupt capability
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    -    - Power → 5V
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         -    - Ground → Ground
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              - ## Final Assembly
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              - 1. **Mount complete arm onto base platform:** Secure the arm assembly to the rotating platform with M4 bolts.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                2. 2. **Cable management:** Route all wires through clips and conduit to prevent tangling.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   3. 3. **Final torque check:** Ensure all fasteners are properly tightened (use appropriate torque specifications for bolt sizes).
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      4. 4. **Protective covers:** Install any protective covers or shrouds as designed in CAD files.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         5. ## Testing & Troubleshooting
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         6. ### Initial Power-On Test
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         7. 1. Apply power to the system
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            2. 2. Verify all LEDs illuminate on the microcontroller and CAN module
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               3. 3. Check that servos respond to control signals
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  4. ### Common Issues
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  5. | Issue | Cause | Solution |
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  6. |-------|-------|----------|
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  7. | Servo not responding | Incorrect GPIO pin | Verify pin configuration in software |
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  8. | Arm movement jerky | Loose joint bolts | Retighten all fasteners |
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  9. | Arm won't rotate | Joint misalignment | Realign arm segments and servo horns |
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  10. | Power supply issues | Inadequate power supply | Upgrade to higher amperage supply |
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  11. | CAN communication errors | Module not detected | Check wiring and SPI configuration |
